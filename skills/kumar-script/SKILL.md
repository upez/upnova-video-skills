# Kumar Method Script Framework

## When to Use
Any request to write a script "like Kumar", a finance-comedy reel, or short-form video script using the @thekumarmethod framework.

Derived from full transcription of 11 @thekumarmethod reels (transcripts: `references/*.txt`, keyed by reel ID; source videos not committed).

## Core mechanics
- **Bit-first, lesson-second** — a cinematic sketch earns the reach; the finance lesson earns the follow.
- **Ritualized catchphrases** — the intro repeats verbatim across videos; it IS the brand.
- **Serialized universe** — recurring characters and arcs; reels are episodes, not standalone tips.
- **Irony armor** — absurdity lets the script attack gurus and give advice without preaching.
- **Deadpan CTA by proxy** — never beg for follows; delegate the ask (wife character, gold stars, flat sign-off).

## The 6-part structure
1. **Cold-open provocation (0–3s)** — a contradiction or absurd claim delivered as a *scene*, not a statement ("Real men don't relax." / "I killed a man named Kumar."). Creates a question the viewer needs resolved.
2. **The cinematic bit (3–15s)** — short film-style sketch playing out the premise: rival gurus, villain arcs, "the haters." High production value signals entertainment-first.
3. **Identity re-intro (the ritual)** — verbatim formula: "My name is Kumar. I'm a retired accountant. And I'm going to [impossible claim]." The impossible claim ("steal your jobs", "biggest accounting influencer in the world") is the running joke.
4. **The actual lesson (mid-reel, compressed)** — 4–5 real points, one sentence each, with concrete stats ($100/mo at 25 → $584k at 65). Education must be legitimately good — it drives saves/shares — but stays deliberately secondary.
5. **Anti-guru positioning** — contrast with Tate/Lopez: "They sold a promise. I can't promise you'll get rich." Trust signals: "We will never make a crypto coin. No website. We are not AI." Irony outside, sincerity inside.
6. **Deadpan outro / CTA** — ends flat and abrupt, never hypey ("Thank you for watching this video file."). CTAs delegated to the wife or gamified ("DM me one thing you learned and I'll get you a gold star"). Tease next installment like a TV show.

## Character roster (one actor, multiple teachers)
- **Kumar** — retired accountant from New York, delusional grandeur, world-class aura.
- **The wife** — deadpan closer: "I am Kumar's wife. We've been married 39 years. Please follow him."
- **Cyrus** — edgy "tough-guy" teacher (negotiation).
- **Rishi** — straight-faced explainer (investing).

## Writing rules
- Every line one sentence or shorter; punchy declaratives.
- Stakes phrased as war/conflict ("they wanted me dead", "preying on my downfall").
- The lesson lands mid-reel, AFTER the bit — never lead with advice.
- End without resolution or summary; abrupt cut is the signature.
- Word budget: ~150–220 words per 45–60s reel.
